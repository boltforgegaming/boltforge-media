How a BoltForge PC gets made

  1. Consult. Tell us your games, budget, and style, by phone or in person.
  2. Spec. We recommend a parts list with a clear, upfront quote.
  3. Build. Hand-assembled with clean cable management.
  4. Test and deliver. Stress-tested and tuned, then picked up in Boise. Shipping starts November 3.
