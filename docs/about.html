Custom Gaming PC FAQ

Pricing & Payment

How much does a BoltForge custom gaming PC cost?

Starting-at list prices, tax included: Core $2,957, Pro $3,858, Pro Max $4,674 and Ultra $10,292. Every build comes with a clear, upfront quote based on the parts you choose, so you know exactly what you are paying before we start.

Is assembly included in the price?

Yes. Our regular assembly fee is $250, and it is already included in our list prices. Launch offers: with our Founder offer, assembly is $149 on builds with a deposit paid by October 31, 2026. With our Buddy Plan, assembly is $99 each when two friends order builds together. The two offers can't be combined.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. We take a 50% deposit when you place your order. Your deposit is refundable until parts are ordered.

Building & Delivery

How does the custom build process work?

Consult, Spec, Build, Test & Deliver: share your games, budget and style; we recommend a parts list with an upfront quote; we hand-assemble your PC; then we stress-test and tune it before pickup or shipping.

How long does a build take?

Typically 7–14 business days after parts arrive; we'll confirm in your quote.

Do you ship, or can I pick up in Boise?

For the first 30 days after our launch on October 4, 2026, we offer local pickup in Boise only, with no shipping. Pickup at the BoltForge showroom inside Boise Computer Care is free. After that, shipping is $79 standard or $149 expedited, and free on orders of $3,000 or more.

Warranty & Returns

What warranty comes with a BoltForge PC?

Every BoltForge build includes an 18-month warranty. See our Warranty page for coverage details and how to request service.

What is your return policy?

Pre-built systems can be returned within 30 days with a 15% restocking fee. Custom builds are returnable only if they are dead or defective. Dead-on-arrival (DOA) issues reported within 14 days are handled free.

BoltForge 18-month warranty

18-month parts and labor warranty

Every BoltForge PC includes an 18-month parts and labor warranty, starting the day you pick up or receive your system. If a covered part fails, we diagnose it, replace or repair it, and re-test your PC at no charge. Replacing a part does not restart the warranty clock.

What is not covered, and how to get service

The warranty does not cover physical or liquid damage, unauthorized modifications, cryptocurrency mining, or overclocking beyond what BoltForge configured. Normal user upgrades like adding memory or storage do not void your coverage.

To request service, call (208) 996-3502, email admin@boltforgegaming.com, or bring your PC to the BoltForge showroom inside Boise Computer Care at 10504 W Fairview Ave, Boise (Friday–Wednesday 10 AM–8 PM, closed Thursday). Have your build serial or order details handy.

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Who we are and what we collect

BoltForge Gaming ("BoltForge," "we," "us") is owned by BoltForge Inc, an Idaho corporation, located at 10504 W Fairview Ave, Boise, ID 83704. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, request a quote, use our configurator, contact us, or buy from us.

We collect:

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, budget, build preferences, and any message or configuration you send through our quote form, configurator, chat, email, or phone.
  • Order and service details: what you buy, deposits and payments, pickup or shipping address, and warranty or repair history. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Website data: basic technical information such as your browser, device, pages viewed, and referring site, collected through cookies and similar tools used by our website platform and analytics.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions, prepare quotes, and build, deliver, and support your PC.
  • To manage leads and customer records. When you submit our quote form or configurator, your details are sent to Zoho CRM, the customer relationship management system we use to track requests, quotes, orders, and warranty service.
  • To process payments, arrange pickup or shipping, and handle returns and warranty claims.
  • To keep our website working, secure, and easy to use, and to understand how visitors use it.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

Email marketing is opt-in

We only send marketing emails, such as new builds, offers, and store news, to people who choose to receive them. Requesting a quote or buying from us does not sign you up for marketing on its own. We will still send messages about your quote, order, or warranty service.

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also opt out anytime by emailing admin@boltforgegaming.com. We honor opt-out requests promptly.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for other companies' advertising.

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed for that purpose. These include Zoho (our website, CRM, email, chat, and business software), payment processors, and shipping carriers. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, customers, or property.

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices and how to reach us

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop marketing emails, at any time. You can also set your browser to block or delete cookies, although some site features may not work as intended.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above.
