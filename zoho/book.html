Services & upgrades

Whether it is a BoltForge build or a PC you bought elsewhere, our hand-build team can upgrade it, clean it up, or track down what is wrong. Bring it to the BoltForge showroom inside Boise Computer Care and we will give you an upfront quote before any work starts.

  • Upgrades: graphics cards, memory, storage, power supplies, cooling, and full platform or case swaps
  • Diagnostics and repair for gaming and custom PCs
  • Cleaning and thermal paste refresh
  • Data transfer and Windows reinstall
  • Onsite setup in the Treasure Valley

Not sure what you need? Describe the problem and we will point you in the right direction.

Every BoltForge build includes an 18-month parts and labor warranty. If something covered fails, warranty repairs are free. Bring your PC in or contact us to start a claim.
