How your build gets made

  1. Configure. Set up your PC above and send it with “Request this build.”
  2. Quote. We confirm parts and pricing and send your upfront quote, usually within 1 business day.
  3. Deposit. A 50% deposit starts your build. It is refundable until parts are ordered.
  4. Build. We build, stress-test, and tune your PC, typically within 7–14 business days after parts arrive. We confirm timing in your quote.
  5. Pickup. Pick it up free at the BoltForge showroom inside Boise Computer Care. Local pickup only for the first 30 days, through November 2. Shipping starts November 3: $79 standard, $149 expedited, free on orders of $3,000 or more.

Every build includes an 18-month parts and labor warranty.
